Detailed Description

A validated SMPTE-style timecode label and its exact frame rate.

Timecodes cover one 24-hour day, from 00:00:00:00 through the last valid label before 24:00:00:00. Drop-frame labels are supported for 30000/1001 and 60000/1001. Instances can only be obtained through validated factories.
